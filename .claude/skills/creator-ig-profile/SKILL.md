# Instagram Profile Audit (ZECRO)

Write everything the audience will see (bio, name field, highlight names, pin rationale) in Romanian. Talk to the user in the language they use.

## ZECRO context (don't ask for these again)
- **Who**: ZECRO S.R.L., sports management agency for Romanian football. Tagline: „Alături de fotbalul românesc spre un viitor mai bun.”
- **Audience**: football clubs and academies (B2B decision makers), young players and their parents, agents and partners, sponsors.
- **Action we want**: visitors tap the link to **zec.ro** (to read the blog or press „Începe un proiect”) or write to office@zec.ro. One link only. Check it's live before recommending it.
- **Offer (4 pillars, use these names)**: Player Advisory, Club Solutions (Club & Academy Solutions), Multi-Club Heritage, Commercial. Players are represented through FIFA-licensed agents.
- **Voice**: transparent, correct, plain, long-term. Calm and professional, not hype. Values: integrity, respect for tradition, sustainable development, supporting and educating players, transparency, respect for communities and fans.
- **Visuals**: background #0E0E0D, accent lime #CAF077, Geist for text. Existing files in `~/Work/ZECRO`: `facebook-profile-image.png`, `zecro-instagram-post-1080x1350`, `zecro-instagram-story-1080x1920.png`, covers and PSDs.
- **Content that already exists**: the blog at zec.ro/blog (4 articles, published weekly from launch), 3 case studies, an infographic carousel on administrative rigor and the 4 pillars.

## Steps
1. Ask only for what's missing: the **handle**, or a screenshot of the profile (Instagram needs a login, so don't try to open it), plus bio, link, highlight names and the current pinned posts if they're not visible. The handle isn't confirmed in the repo (`CONTEXT.md` noted no Instagram account as of Oct 2026), so never assume one.
2. Score 1–10 with one line each: name field (searchable keyword?), bio (who it's for + what they get + proof), link, highlights, top 3 pinned posts, profile photo.
3. Rewrite:
   - 3 bio options, max 150 characters, each with a clear next step (usually the zec.ro link).
   - Name field (max 64 characters) with a search keyword, e.g. „management sportiv” or „agenție fotbal”.
   - Highlight names and covers (max 5), drawn from what exists: projects, services/pillars, blog, player guide, contact.
   - Which 3 posts to pin and why (prefer the case studies, the 4 pillars and the best blog article).
4. End with a short, ordered to-do list (what to change first).

## Rules
- Plain words, no emoji walls. Max 2 emojis in a bio.
- Never invent proof. If there's no number to show, leave [PROOF].
- **Proof that is confirmed and can be used**: three real projects (A.S.P.I., ACSM Cetatea 1932 Suceava, Gloria Ultra), Cetatea Suceava's promotion to Liga 2, A.S.P.I.'s registration in Liga 4, agents licensed by FIFA, and the transparent, administrative-first approach. Prefer describing the work over claiming results.
- **Never use or imply**:
  - the founders' names;
  - kit or equipment brands (Adidas, JAKO, Joma);
  - invented numbers, percentages or rankings;
  - that A.S.P.I. is a „rebuilt” or „reactivated” old club (it is a new club built on the spirit and tradition of Politehnica Iași, evolved from Steaua Magică);
  - that Gloria Ultra is still active or in Liga 3 (the collaboration ended when the financiers moved the club toward mini-football; mention it neutrally, once, only if needed).
- Don't promise results to players or parents (no „garantăm transferuri”), and no unverifiable superlatives („cea mai bună agenție”).
- Author of public text is „ZECRO”, not a person.
